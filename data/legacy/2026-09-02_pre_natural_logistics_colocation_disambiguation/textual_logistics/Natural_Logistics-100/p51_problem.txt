In the Logistics game, the initial setup includes several packages, trucks, cities, locations, and airports. Packages are labeled objX1, objX2, and objX3 for each integer X from 1 through 9, with trucks tru1 through tru9 and cities cit1 through cit9. The locations are pos1 through pos9 and apt1 through apt9, with apt1 through apt9 designated as airports and apn1 through apn3 designated as airplanes. Initially, apn1 and apn3 are stationed at airport apt9, while apn2 is located at apt4. Trucks and packages are placed at the corresponding positions: for each integer X from 1 through 9, truX and packages objX1, objX2, and objX3 are at posX. Each location pos1 to pos9 is situated within cities cit1 to cit9, respectively, as are the airports apt1 to apt9.
