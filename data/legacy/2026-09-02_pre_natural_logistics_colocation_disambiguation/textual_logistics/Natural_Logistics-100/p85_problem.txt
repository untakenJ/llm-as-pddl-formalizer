In the Logistics game, we start with six packages and six cities. For each city cityX, where X is an integer from 1 through 6, its exact normal-location identifier is cityX-1 and its exact airport identifier is cityX-2. Each city has one truck: truck6 is initially at city6-1, truck5 at city5-1, truck4 at city4-1, truck3 at city3-1, truck2 at city2-1, and truck1 at city1-1. There are two airplanes, plane2 and plane1, both initially stationed at city4-2. The packages are distributed across the cities: package6 is currently at city3-1, package5 is at city4-2, and both package4 and package3 are at city1-1. Meanwhile, package2 is at city1-2, and package1 is at city2-1. The challenge is to rearrange these packages to achieve the following goal: package6 should be delivered to city1-2, package5 to city6-2, package4 to city3-2, package3 to city6-1, package2 to city6-2, and package1 remains at city2-1.
