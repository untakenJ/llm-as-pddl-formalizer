Initial setup for the logistics game includes, for each X from 1 to 12, the three packages objX1, objX2, and objX3, and includes 12 trucks labeled from tru1 to tru12. There are also 12 cities labeled from cit1 to cit12, each with a position and airport location. Every airplane (apn1, apn2, and apn3) is initially stationed as follows: apn1 and apn2 are at apt12, and apn3 is at apt7. For each X from 1 to 12, posX and aptX are locations in citX, and aptX is an airport. Specifically, initially, for each X from 1 to 12, truck truX and packages objX1, objX2, and objX3 are at posX. The goal is to rearrange these packages such that specific packages end up at certain locations.
